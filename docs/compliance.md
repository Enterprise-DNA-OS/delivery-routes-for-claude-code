# Work-time and dispatch checks

Checked 23 October 2026. New Zealand rules only. Apply these to drivers subject to the Land Transport Rule: Work Time and Logbooks 2007. Small passenger-service exceptions, exemptions, approved variations, ferries and Australian rules are outside this base.

Source: [NZTA work-time and logbook requirements](https://nzta.govt.nz/business/commercial-safety/work-time-and-logbook-requirements).

| Check | Recorded threshold | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| NZ-WT-5.5 | At most 330 work minutes before at least 30 continuous rest minutes | Reports the interval crossing the threshold |
| NZ-WT-13 | At most 780 work minutes in a cumulative work day, reset by 600 continuous rest minutes | Reports excess recorded work |
| NZ-WT-70 | At most 4200 work minutes in a cumulative work period, reset by 1440 continuous rest minutes | Reports excess recorded work |
| COVERAGE | Every driver needs complete work history | Always reports unknown coverage, even with no threshold breach |
| DISPATCH-REVIEW | Current licence, current inspection and confirmed planning inputs | Expired dates and unconfirmed imports need attention |

Driving, loading and other work all count. Gaps are treated as rest only for calculations on this recorded history. A gap does not prove the driver rested. Include work for other employers. Missing prior history means the initial day and period totals are lower bounds. A passing threshold calculation is never a legal compliance certificate. The report does not validate every cumulative 24-hour day boundary or prescribed logbook field, and is not an approved electronic logbook. Keep the legally required logbook separately.

The planner budgets breaks before a travel or service segment would cross 330 work minutes, counts waiting as work, caps planned work at 780 minutes and includes return travel. These are planning checks, independent of the actual work register. Release is a dispatcher assertion, not certification that actual work history permits driving. Reconcile the actual logbook, prior rest, other employment, road access, vehicle class and load restraints before releasing a route. Planned rest requires a suitable stationary rest location.

Licence and inspection dates are operator-entered evidence. This base does not check a government register. No notification, enforcement, payment or driver messaging occurs.
