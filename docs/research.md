# Selection evidence

Checked 23 October 2026. Family: delivery-route-planning. The registry has fleet maintenance, freight forwarding, transport management and field sales builds, but no delivery route-planning anchor. ConductorOne and TrackOnline, tonight's earlier builds, cover different families.

[OptimoRoute pricing](https://optimoroute.com/pricing/) lists Lite at 35.10 and Pro at 44.10 per driver per month with annual billing. Its FAQ gives Pro monthly billing at 49 per driver. A 50-driver Pro annual scenario is 26,460 a year before tax. This is arithmetic from the vendor rate, not a verified customer invoice or a claim about what an existing Enterprise DNA customer pays. Pricing is shown in dollars on the vendor site; confirm billing currency in a quote before purchasing.

Scores in order: annual bill, data-shaped, our buyers, search demand, one-session buildable, import path. These are editorial judgements, not measured market statistics.

| Candidate | Scores | Total | Selection reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| OptimoRoute | 3, 4, 2, 3, 4, 5 | 21 | Public per-driver price and documented raw export, useful offline dispatch workflows |
| Onfleet | 3, 3, 2, 3, 4, 5 | 20 | Public Scale price 1,349 monthly, greater dependency on live driver services |
| Routific | 1, 4, 2, 3, 4, 4 | 18 | Public starting price 150 monthly for 1,000 orders, lower bill at entry |

Other sources opened: [Onfleet pricing](https://onfleet.com/pricing/), [Routific pricing](https://www.routific.com/pricing/), [OptimoRoute export help](https://help.optimoroute.com/hc/en-us/articles/27630278304404-Export-routes-to-Excel), [Capterra Australia alternatives](https://www.capterra.com.au/alternatives/161579/optimoroute).

Search results contained several vendor comparison pages and an Australian review directory. Search volume was not measured. Local customer-context searches did not establish an OptimoRoute purchase, so buyer fit scores only 2. No customer names or confidential records appear in the demo or page.

The incumbent supplies actual optimization and live services, not merely screens. This build replaces the owned dispatch record and documented offline workflows. It does not claim feature parity. Ten useful questions are demonstrated without asserting that OptimoRoute is incapable of answering them.
